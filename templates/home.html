About This Project
Our Mission

PF-LU aims to eliminate introduced predators from the Lincoln University campus, creating safe habitat for native birds and wildlife.

Trap Lines

Multiple trap lines across campus, each consisting of various trap types — A24, DOC 150, DOC 200, Flipping Timmy, and more.

Community Effort

Operators and Observers work together to monitor traps, record catches, and submit incidental observations of native wildlife.

How It Works
  1. 1
    Register as an Observer

    Create your account to start contributing to predator control efforts.

  2. 2
    Get Assigned to a Trap Line

    An Admin will assign you to one or more trap lines on campus.

  3. 3
    Check Traps & Record Results

    Visit your assigned traps, record catches, rebaiting, and trap conditions.

  4. 4
    Submit Incidental Observations

    Report any wildlife sightings or unusual activity you encounter in the field.

User Roles
  • Observer View trap lines and submit observations
  • Operator Check traps, record catches, manage assigned lines
  • Admin Manage users, lines, traps, and all system data
